Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

Melkar, LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates the PocSomm mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use PocSomm.

Information We Collect

Camera Images

When you use PocSomm's scanning features (wine list scanning, bottle scanning, or food menu scanning), the App captures photos using your device's camera. These images are processed to extract wine and food information.

How Images and Text Are Processed

To analyze a scan, captured images are sent to Google's Gemini API via Firebase for processing. The same applies to wine lists you import (PDF pages, photos, or the text of a web page), dish names and recipe details you use for pairings, and questions you ask PocSomm.

This content is sent solely to produce the wine and food information you asked for. We do not store it on any server. It is transmitted directly from your device to Google and is subject to Google's data handling policies.

Your Taste Profile

If you create a taste profile, it is built and stored on your device. To tailor answers to your taste, a short text summary of it (for example, "prefers light, fresh reds and crisp whites; avoids oaky Chardonnay") is sent to Google's Gemini API along with bottle scans, food-pairing requests, and questions you ask PocSomm. It is never sent with wine-list scans; matching a wine list to your taste happens on your device. The summary contains no name, contact details, or other identifying information.

If you choose to type wines you have loved or disliked while setting up your profile, those wine names are sent to Google's Gemini API so they can be matched to grapes, regions, and styles. Nothing is added to your profile until you confirm it.

As with all AI requests, this is sent solely to produce the answer you asked for and is not stored by PocSomm on any server.

Links You Import

When you paste a link to a restaurant wine list or a recipe, the App downloads that page directly from the website so it can be analyzed. As with any web request, that website receives your device's IP address and a standard request header. PocSomm does not send the link or the page to us.

Protecting the Service From Abuse

To make sure requests to the AI service come from the genuine PocSomm app on a genuine Apple device, the App uses Firebase App Check with Apple's App Attest. This sends a cryptographic device attestation to Apple and Google. It contains no personal information and is not used to identify or track you.

Location Data

If you grant permission, PocSomm uses your approximate location (accuracy of roughly 100 meters, not precise GPS) to help you find nearby wine shops. When you choose to search for wine shops, your approximate coordinates are included in an Apple Maps link that opens in the Maps app. PocSomm operates no servers of its own and does not transmit your location to us; wine-shop searches are handled by Apple Maps and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. You may set a saved home location to use instead of your current location.

Locally Stored Data

The following data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Scan history: Wine and food scan results (stored in app documents)
  • Wine ratings: Your personal wine ratings (stored in app documents)
  • Pairing history: Your saved food-pairing searches (stored in app documents)
  • Taste profile: Your tasting-game answers, stated preferences, and what the App has learned from your ratings and feedback (stored in app documents)
  • To-try list: Wines you have marked to try (stored in app documents)
  • Answer cache: Recent sommelier answers, kept for a limited time so repeat questions are instant (stored in UserDefaults)
  • Credit balance: Your scan credit balance (stored in the iOS Keychain)
  • Preferences: App settings such as language, currency market, and display preferences (stored in UserDefaults)

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number).
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.
  • We do not sell or share your data with data brokers.

Third-Party Services

AI Provider

Images and text you choose to analyze, and the taste-profile summary described above, are sent to Google for processing, subject to its privacy policy:

  • Google (Gemini API via Firebase, and Firebase App Check): Google Privacy Policy

Apple StoreKit and App Attest

PocSomm uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases and offer codes. Purchase transactions and offer-code redemptions are handled entirely by Apple; PocSomm only records on your device which purchases have already been credited. App Attest (see above) is also provided by Apple. Both are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete your scan history, ratings, and pairing history at any time in the App, and you can view, edit, or reset your taste profile in Settings. Settings → Delete All App Data deletes the App's data on your device, including your taste profile and To-try list. Your scan credit balance is stored in the iOS Keychain and is retained across reinstalls; it contains no personal information and is not transmitted anywhere.

Children's Privacy

PocSomm contains information about alcoholic beverages and is intended for users of legal drinking age (18 years or older). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
