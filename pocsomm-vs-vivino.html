PocSomm vs Vivino for restaurant wine lists

Checked 17 November 2026 · By the PocSomm team

We make PocSomm. Everything about Vivino below comes from Vivino's own website and App Store listing, checked on 17 November 2026, and we say plainly where Vivino is better. Prices are for the US. If something is out of date, tell us at support@pocsomm.com.

Vivino tells you whether people like a wine. PocSomm tells you whether the restaurant's price for it is fair. Vivino's list scanner shows the community star rating for every wine and needs Premium ($4.99 a month or $47.90 a year). PocSomm shows every wine's estimated shop price, markup and a value verdict, with pay-as-you-go credits and no account. Vivino is far bigger and better for ratings and buying wine; PocSomm is built for the moment at the table.

Where Vivino is better

  • Real community ratings. Millions of people have rated wines on Vivino; PocSomm has no community.
  • Buying wine. Vivino's marketplace lets you order the wine you liked.
  • Your own wine history. Ratings, a cellar and social features, plus a match score based on your taste.
  • Everywhere. Android and the web as well as iPhone.
  • A mature label scanner used for years, by a well-known brand.

Where PocSomm is better at the restaurant table

  • It answers "is this price fair?" Every wine gets an estimated shop price, its markup and a verdict, and the list can be sorted by value. Vivino shows ratings, not value against the list price.
  • No subscription. Credits are pay-as-you-go; Vivino's list scanner needs Premium.
  • Food pairing with the actual menu. Photograph the food menu, pick each person's dish, and PocSomm ranks the whole wine list for that dinner.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Foreign lists. 11 languages and 21 currency markets: read an Italian list in English, with prices estimated for the market you're in.

One honest caveat: PocSomm's prices are AI estimates, not live shop prices. For the bottle you settle on, a quick check on Wine-Searcher or a local shop's site is worth it.

Which should you use?

  • You want to know which wines people love, and maybe buy one later: Vivino.
  • You want to avoid overpaying, or pick the best wine for tonight's dishes: PocSomm.
  • Both: they don't conflict. Rate and buy with Vivino, check the list with PocSomm.

Frequently asked questions

Is PocSomm a Vivino alternative?

For restaurant wine lists, partly. Vivino shows how its community rates each wine; PocSomm shows whether the restaurant's price is fair and which bottle suits your dinner.

Is Vivino's wine list scanner free?

No. Vivino's own October 2026 guide calls it a Premium-only feature: $4.99 a month or $47.90 a year in the US.

Does Vivino tell you if restaurant wine is overpriced?

Not directly. Its list scanner shows star ratings. You can look up Vivino's own price for a wine and compare it yourself, or use our markup calculator.
