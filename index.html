How pricing works

PocSomm is free to download and comes with credits to get you started. Each scan uses a credit or two depending on its size.

Need more? Top up anytime with one-time credit packs. No subscription, no account, no surprises — and your remaining credits never expire.

Your data, your business

Private by design

No account. No sign-up. Your scans, ratings, and history live only on your device.

When AI reads a photo, only the image is sent to Google's Gemini service to extract the wine details — it is never stored on our servers (we don't run any) and never sold. No ads, no cross-app tracking.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is PocSomm?

PocSomm uses advanced AI to read labels and lists and to estimate prices, ratings, and pairings. It's remarkably good — but AI can make mistakes, so always verify prices and details before buying.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. PocSomm analyzes your photos in the cloud, so you'll need a connection to scan and to ask the sommelier.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account, and your scans, ratings, and history stay on your device. Photos are sent only to the AI to read them — never stored on our servers, never sold, with no ads or tracking.

What does it cost?

PocSomm is free to download with credits to get you started. When you need more, you can buy one-time credit packs — there's no subscription.

Which currencies and regions does it work with?

PocSomm estimates prices in the market you choose and works with wine lists and bottles from all over the world.

How old do I need to be?

PocSomm contains information about alcoholic beverages and is intended for adults of legal drinking age (18+).

Who we are

A husband-and-wife labor of love

PocSomm was born from over 30 years of shared passion for wine. Along the way we’ve had the pleasure of learning from truly exceptional sommeliers — some of whom have become dear friends. But we’ve also sat at tables where the wine guidance fell short.

PocSomm is our way to bridge that gap: not to replace a great sommelier, but to put a knowledgeable partner in your pocket for the moments when there isn’t one around.
